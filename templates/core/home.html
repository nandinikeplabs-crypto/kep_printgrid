Why Xerox Shops & Customers Love PrintGrid

Built from the ground up for security, speed, and hardware resilience.

1. Permanent QR Code

One shop QR code that never expires. Add, replace, or reassign printers without ever needing to print a new QR stand.

2. Zero-Knowledge Encryption

Documents are encrypted in the customer's browser using AES-256-GCM and unwrapped only by the local shop connector. The cloud backend never sees plaintext files.

3. Intelligent Spooler Routing

Automatically dispatches B&W jobs to fast monochrome printers and color jobs to photo-capable copiers with automated failover.
